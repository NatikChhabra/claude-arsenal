# Pack template

## The bit pack (the file he reads)
```
# V<n> · <TOPIC> · your bit pack   (story script: <link>)
## ACT 1: <the topic's own milestone>  (stop point: <where>; trap: <the one setting or save trap>)
1. <bit>  <!-- [src: <transcript> <mm:ss> | <his words>] -->
2. ...
## ACT 2 ...
```
Game order, 1-3 bits per act, every act gets a beat. They are his pacing guide.

## The hidden plan (the edit reads it; he doesn't)
```
## V<n> · target length · LIVE | VOICEOVER | HYBRID (+ watch-back voice-over after recording)
One video, one story (start -> the topic's own goal) · facts: <primer> (flag what to check on screen) · policy ·
demand slot yes/no · the 3 title + thumbnail pairs · 3 working titles (on the channel template, true from minute one)

## THE PLAN ON ONE SCREEN
- the frame (the topic's situation as a local life situation) · the spine (the topic's own number, said every few
  minutes) · the running gag (3 escalating beats + a payoff, bit numbers) · the callback pair · the twist to watch for ·
  "beyond them" (what this video does that none of the role models do) · recording plan (2 blocks with a break, max 2.5 h)

## BEFORE YOU PRESS RECORD (5 min)
- install, settings and saving traps · voice chain on · read the pack once, star 8-10, rewrite into your words, never read
  a block straight off · 3 THUMBNAIL SHOTS (HUD off, the subject big, the moment just BEFORE the payoff), written into
  the recording-day checklist he does see

## STORY MAP | Act | On screen | What the act is about | Bits | Bridge (his live line or a voice-over line, picked at the edit) |
cold open (his funniest real moment) · promise (0:10-0:45) · acts = the topic's own milestones (4-6 min each in the cut),
each ending on a turn ("but...") · payoff (the goal lands + callback + gag payoff, his live sign-off)

## LIVE RULES   (improv between the bits: what to say before a purchase or build, at every big number; a "Never:" line)
## WHEN YOU RUN OUT OF WORDS   (things to describe, rate, compare, the local version of what's on screen)
## EXTRAS WORTH A DETOUR (all real, all optional) · ## CLIP-THAT MOMENTS (Shorts: cut from this recording only)
## AFTER RECORDING
"recording done" + where the best moments were + which bits he used. Editor notes (edit rules: skill design, references/edit.md): the format's order, mascot corner,
captions (his script; topic words as the topic spells them), the gag beats and the callback pair are structural (if he
said them, they stay), thumbnail moments, the title/thumbnail test.
```
