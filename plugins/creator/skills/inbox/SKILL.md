# inbox (an email connector)

status: beta

Hard rule: read, sort, DRAFT only. An email goes out only on the creator's explicit OK for THAT email, and he sends it
himself. Never send, reply, forward, or touch any account other than the channel's business inbox.

1. Search for new mail since the last triage (note the date in the project's hand-off).
2. Label: `deal` (a paid offer from a real brand), `collab`, `platform` (YouTube, Meta, Google notices), `spam/scam`
   (fake sponsorships, paid "promotion" offers, crypto, anything asking for logins, money, a file download or a
   "test" install).
3. A real deal: check the brand (its site, its past creator deals), what it asks (integration length, usage rights,
   exclusivity, deadlines), the pay if named. Mark red flags (pay-to-join, vague brand, rights forever, upfront fees).
4. Draft a reply in his voice (short, plain, no stock phrases; the project's voice checker if it has one). Never a rate,
   date or commitment he hasn't given.
5. Tell him in bullets: each email worth his time, the draft's subject, what he must decide.
