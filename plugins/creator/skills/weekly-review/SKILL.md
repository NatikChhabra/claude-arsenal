# weekly-review (same day every week)

status: beta

## 1. Pull
The project's analytics tools if it has them (YouTube + Instagram, every video), else Studio and Insights by hand into
the metrics ledger. Public numbers for other channels: `yt-dlp --flat-playlist -J <channel>` (public listings only,
never a logged-in session).

## 2. Read (each with its number)
- **Bars:** skill `growth-funnel` §1 + §6 (target-country share, viewed vs swiped, skip, CTR, 0:30, APV, sends), plus
  >= 10 subs per 1k views.
- **CTR at 24 h** against the channel's OWN videos at similar impressions, never a raw average.
- **North star:** returning viewers (Studio audience segments) + APV; decide on 3-5 video trends, never one video.
- **Outliers:** the channel's top 3 / bottom 3 vs its median + the week's small-creator outliers in the niche (skill
  `yt-viral`); one line on what the winners share.
- **Pace:** the goal's checkpoints (the project's subscriber milestones and dates). Behind = say by how much and the one
  lever that closes it.
- Shorts: engaged views + viewed vs swiped + skip rate per hook pattern; Reels: sends, skip rate.

## 3. Act
- **FAST FOLLOW-UP:** a long in its first 14 days at >= 3x the channel median at the same day-1/3/7 checkpoint = the
  next video stays on that topic (Part 2, banked footage) or runs the same premise on a new topic, out within 2 weeks,
  ahead of the lineup.
- A Short or Reel at >= 5x the channel's Short/Reel median: say so; the next Shorts come from that topic + moment type,
  and a long on it is a pitch to the creator with the numbers.
- **Lineup:** under 2 videos planned, or no Shorts runway = a dated plan in the hand-off (what he records and when, his
  one review sitting before any time away).
- **Part 2** goes up when Part 1's day-7 views >= 2x the median, OR its 0:30 >= the channel's best, OR the poll or
  comments say yes. Settle the Part 2 field either way (it unlocks the raw cleanup).
- A long under the median at day 7: a repackage proposal (the Test & Compare winner or a new pair; his OK for the exact
  text).
- After 8-10 videos: cut the weakest pillar. Check the demand slot (skill `topic-pick`).
- New-topic radar: the shortlist step of skill `topic-pick`, logged with the pick marked.

## 4. Log + tell him
A dated entry at the top of the review log: what worked, what lost, the ONE change for the next video (where it lands:
tool, gate, skill, style log) + the number it should move + its check date. Update the plan and the creator's dashboard
if it moved. Him: 3-5 bullets.
