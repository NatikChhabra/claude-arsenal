# Design brief

status: draft

## Brief fields
- What and where it lives: in-game billboard/poster (resolution limits), Discord, Instagram, YouTube thumbnail,
  album/single cover (3000x3000), merch.
- Who it is for and the one action they should take.
- The brand it belongs to (character, gang, business, server event): look for `brands/<name>/BRAND.md` first.
- References (2-3), must-have text (exact), deadline.
- What would make it fail (e.g. looks like a template, unreadable at phone size).
Write it to `design/<slug>/BRIEF.md`, then hand to `rp-graphics` or `brand-kit`.
