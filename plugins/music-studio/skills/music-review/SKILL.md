# Music review

status: draft

Score each 1-5; anything under 4 gets fixed and re-scored before delivery.
- Hook: chorus memorable after one listen.
- Lyrics: specific, natural, no AI-stock lines; pronunciation clean in every language used.
- Vocal: no robotic artefacts, breaths and timing feel human; blind listener doesn't flag "AI".
- Guitar: his part is audible and central (it is his song).
- Mix: vocal clear on a phone speaker; loudness numbers in MIX.md.
- Video (if any): cuts land on the beat map; 9:16 version re-laid out; no flash violations.
- Rights: CREDITS.md complete; licences allow the intended use.
