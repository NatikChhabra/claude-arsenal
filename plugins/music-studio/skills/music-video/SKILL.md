# Music video

status: draft

Music videos are the top priority of this arsenal. Building blocks to rebuild here: a beat map,
kinetic lyrics rendered in headless Chromium, and a native 9:16 re-layout (see `research/02_music_video_pipeline.md`).

## Steps
1. Treatment: one visual idea that carries the song (thesis), the character's arc, locations in the RP world, and
   3+ held moments. Shot list per section.
2. Footage: in-game capture plan (Rockstar Editor or server-allowed cinematic tools, recording at the target fps),
   with friends/characters cast and scheduled. Check the server's rules for filming in public RP spaces.
3. Beat map of the song (beats, bars, drops) so cuts and effects land on the music.
4. Edit: cuts on beats, speed ramps on drops, lyric text where it helps; colour grade per section.
5. Exports: 16:9 master; 9:16 cut for Shorts/Reels/TikTok, re-laid out (not just cropped); thumbnail via design-studio.
6. QA: flashes <= 3 per second (photosensitivity), lyrics on screen long enough to read, audio = the mastered file.
