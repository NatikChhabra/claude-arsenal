# Produce and arrange

status: draft

## Options
- DAW control from Claude: Ableton MCP (`ahujasid/ableton-mcp`, MIT; Live 10+) or a REAPER MCP server (TRIAL, see
  registry). Free DAW choice if he has none: REAPER (cheap licence) or Cakewalk/Waveform free.
- Generated stems: ACE-Step 1.5 Vocal2BGM / track separation, or Suno stems, as starting points to replace or layer.

## Steps
1. Section map from the song sheet: energy per section (1-10), what enters and leaves.
2. Drums and bass first, locked to his guitar's grid; then keys/pads; then ear candy at section changes.
3. Leave space for the vocal (cut 1-4 kHz clutter where the vocal sits).
4. Bounce stems per instrument for `mix-master`.
